OUR DIFFERENCE

Built to a higher standard. Not just a higher price.

For more than four decades, Lovett Homes has built communities that have become some of Houston's most desirable addresses. At Lake Pointe Green, luxury finishes come standard — timeless architecture, built by teams with decades of experience.

Luxury finishes, as standard.

Every Lake Pointe Green home is designed with the details buyers usually pay extra for: soaring 12-foot ceilings, white oak wide-plank floors, chef-inspired kitchens and spa-inspired owner's suites.

Outside, painted or slurried brick, premium roofing and landscaping composed as one continuous plan give every street a timeless, finished look.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Inside and out, the details are standard.

  1. Timeless Exteriors

    West U and European-inspired architecture with gas lanterns, gracious balconies and gently curving streetscapes. Garages and trash pickup sit off rear alleyways, keeping the fronts of homes clean.

    EXTERIOR

    INCLUDED AS STANDARD

    • Premium slate, metal or composition roof (per plan)
    • Brick and siding exteriors — brick can be painted or slurried
    • Custom front entry door
    • French doors that open the living space to the outdoors
    • Landscaping and irrigation composed as a single, continuous plan

  2. Interior Living

    Open-concept living spaces with soaring ceilings and walls of glass that open to balconies, rooftop terraces and outdoor living areas.

    INTERIORS

    INCLUDED AS STANDARD

    • 12-foot ceilings on the main level
    • Elevated ceiling detail and custom trim throughout
    • 8-foot solid-core interior doors
    • White oak European wide-plank flooring
    • 10-foot glass doors up to 16 feet wide (per plan)

  3. Kitchen & Owner's Retreat

    Chef-inspired kitchens built around a large center island, and owner's suites designed as a private sanctuary.

    KITCHEN + SUITE

    INCLUDED AS STANDARD

    • High-end stainless steel appliances
    • Large center island with custom soft-close cabinetry
    • Quartz, marble or quartzite countertops with under-cabinet lighting
    • Spa-inspired bath with freestanding tub and oversized shower
    • Custom walk-in closet with built-in organization and dual vanities

  4. Comfort & Efficiency

    Systems chosen for lower maintenance and greater comfort year-round in the Gulf Coast climate.

    PERFORMANCE

    INCLUDED AS STANDARD

    • High-efficiency HVAC
    • Smart thermostat
    • Tankless water heater
    • Energy-efficient insulation package
    • Tight building envelope and low-maintenance exterior
