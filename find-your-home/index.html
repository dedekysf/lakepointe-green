THE SITE

A distinctive site with a new purpose.

On the former Fluor corporate campus, Lake Pointe Green is designed as a community — homes, parks, green spaces and walkable connections considered together, with majestic oaks and an expanded trail network along Brooks Lake and Oyster Creek, and kayaking and rowing on the lake. Whole Foods Market, Life Time Fitness and Sugar Land Town Square are minutes away, and US-59/I-69 and Highway 6 put the Energy Corridor and the Texas Medical Center under 30 minutes away.

Who is developing Lake Pointe Green?

Lake Pointe Green is developed and built by Lovett Homes, a Houston-based developer with more than four decades of experience in residential development and transformative mixed-use projects, whose work has earned a first-place Urban Land Institute Development Distinction Award. The community represents a shared vision with the City of Sugar Land.

Where is Lake Pointe Green?

Lake Pointe Green is on the approximately 53-acre former Fluor corporate campus on Brooks Lake in Sugar Land, Texas, just off US-59/I-69 and Highway 6. The model home is at 1 Fluor Daniel Dr, Sugar Land, TX 77478.

How far is Lake Pointe Green from the Texas Medical Center and the Energy Corridor?

Both are under 30 minutes by car in typical off-peak weekday traffic. Houston Methodist Sugar Land Hospital is about 5 minutes away and Sugar Land Regional Airport about 10 minutes.

Which schools serve Lake Pointe Green?

Lake Pointe Green is in Fort Bend ISD, currently zoned to Highlands Elementary, Dulles Middle School and Dulles High School. Zoning is subject to change, so verify with Fort Bend ISD before closing.

How large are the homes at Lake Pointe Green?

Homes are two- and three-story designs from about 2,300 to more than 5,000 square feet, with 2- or 3-car garages off rear alleys. Featured Phase 1 plans range from 2,283 to 4,351 square feet.

How many homes are in Lake Pointe Green?

Phase 1 is a limited release of 97 homes, out of more than 300 homes planned across the community.

Do the homes have space for a home office?

Yes. Every featured plan has a place to work: a first-floor study (Lot 30), a ground-floor suite with its own bath (Lot 18), a finished third floor (Lots 22 and 24), or six bedrooms across three levels (Lot 23).

What comes standard in a Lake Pointe Green home?

Standard features include 12-foot main-level ceilings, white oak European wide-plank floors, 8-foot solid-core doors, 10-foot glass doors up to 16 feet wide (per plan), quartz, marble or quartzite countertops, high-end stainless appliances, a spa-inspired owner's bath, high-efficiency HVAC, a smart thermostat and a tankless water heater.

How much do homes at Lake Pointe Green cost?

Phase 1 pricing is available from the sales team on request. Call Greg Burton at 713-503-4582 or Kathryn Yao at 713-340-9010.

Does Lake Pointe Green work with buyer's agents?

Yes. Lovett Homes honors a 3% co-op commission on registered transactions. Register your client before their first visit.

How do I tour Lake Pointe Green?

Visit the model home at 1 Fluor Daniel Dr, Sugar Land, Monday to Saturday 10am–6pm or Sunday 12pm–5pm, or schedule a private appointment with the sales team online.
