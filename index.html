THE VISION

An established city. A distinctive new neighborhood.

In a city where opportunities for significant new residential development have become increasingly limited, Lake Pointe Green offers something exceptional: the opportunity to own a new home in an established, highly desirable Sugar Land location.

Situated on the former Fluor corporate campus, this approximately 53-acre redevelopment is being thoughtfully transformed into a new residential neighborhood, with parks, green spaces, walkable connections and access to the conveniences that make Sugar Land such a desirable place to live.

ON THE WATER

Life framed by the water.

Lake Pointe Green is wrapped by Brooks Lake and Oyster Creek. Trails follow the shoreline beneath majestic oaks, kayakers and rowers glide past in the morning, and walkable streets open onto water views. Fort Bend ISD schools, US-59/I-69 and Highway 6 are all close by.
